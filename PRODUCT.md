# Product

## Register
brand

## Users and purpose
Super 44 is a developer portfolio for recruiters, engineering leads, and collaborators. Visitors need to identify the developer, inspect evidence of work, and reach contact without navigating a game. Source: docs/01-PROJECT-VISION.md.

## Brand personality
Mechanical, composed, human. A private racing workshop at blue hour, with warm practical light and purposeful engineering. Super 44 is a fictional identity, without official team affiliation.

## Principles
Evidence leads. Native navigation stays familiar. The HTML portfolio is complete independently of its decorative canvas. Failure preserves reading and navigation. Never invent personal facts or project results.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA; keyboard access, selectable HTML, static posters, and reduced-motion support are requirements. This vertical slice is a clearly labeled prototype, not a completed personal portfolio.

## Current approved scope
