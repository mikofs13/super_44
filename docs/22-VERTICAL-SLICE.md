# Implemented vertical slice

This is the two-pose greybox milestone, not the complete personal portfolio. Personal content remains intentionally absent rather than fabricated. See [verification](verification/REPORT.md).

## Boundaries

- `src/app/page.tsx`: build-rendered HTML content, native anchors, semantic navigation.
- `src/experience/SceneBoundary.tsx`: eligibility, saved view, session motion consent, timeout, model cancellation, retry, error containment, and poster ownership. Imports the scene only after eligibility and two paint opportunities.
- `src/experience/ExperienceCanvas.tsx`: optional Three/R3F/GSAP bundle, model fetch/parse, primitive garage, measured scroll controller, and sole camera writer. No Drei, external textures, postprocessing, shadows, or physics.
- `src/experience/policy.ts`: independently tested mode precedence and progress clamping.
- `scripts/generate-car.mjs`: reproducible original glTF with embedded geometry. The model has no external dependencies or decoders.

## Loading contract

Idle -> loading after eligibility -> ready only after a successful render. Request/parse/render errors or lost context -> failed. Eight seconds without a frame -> timed-out. Both failure states retain the poster and offer one new attempt per explicit activation. There is no automatic retry. Static aborts the active request, removes the canvas, and disposes model resources. Imports already downloaded cannot be undone, but a cold saved-Static visit requests no optional JS or model.

The R3F renderer handles scene reconciliation and renderer cleanup; the boundary explicitly owns the loaded glTF geometries/materials. Garage geometry is scoped to its mounted scene. ScrollTrigger, resize observer, window listeners, pending refresh frame, and model request are canceled by their respective owners.

## Scope choices

Use the documented 24m x 32m shell with a car at the origin and +Z nose. The hero camera is (8, 4.2, -10), looking at (-0.3, 0.65, 0); Projects is (9.5, 3.4, 4), looking at (0, 0.65, 1.4). These adapted poses keep the small original car legible in the side panel. Native section measurements interpolate both values reversibly, with no damping, pins, or travel animation on navigation.

Desktop Auto enables the scene; compact/coarse-pointer and detectable data-saving Auto keep the poster. Explicit 3D may opt in. Reduced motion takes precedence unless explicitly enabled for this visit. Static always wins. The visible selector exposes Static/3D; Auto is the unsaved default.

Use fixed Low/Medium DPR caps (1 / 1.25). Adaptive frame-time quality tiers, six chapters, finished assets, real case studies, résumé/contact details, and production release remain later work. No FPS or field Web Vitals claim is made by this slice.

## Asset provenance

The car is original geometry generated by this repository; the garage is original procedural geometry. `garage-poster.webp` is a capture of that scene, not third-party art. No sponsor or team marks are used. Font packages are Barlow Condensed and Source Sans 3 from Fontsource, pinned in the lockfile. Their OFL notices are preserved in `public/fonts/`. Both font families are served locally; no runtime font-provider request is made.
